Leadership

Founder & CEO

When I began my journey as a young mechanical engineer, I saw many talented classmates graduate without the practical design skills industries actually expect. I experienced the same challenge - until I immersed myself in advanced CAD training and discovered how the right guidance can transform a career.

CDTC was born to close that gap. We believe core-engineering students deserve affordable, world-class design education that makes them truly job-ready.

In just four intensive days, we bring real-world practices into the classroom, certify students, and help them see that engineering isn't just theory — it's innovation, creativity, and opportunity.

Over the next five years, CDTC aims to partner with hundreds of institutions across India and eventually take our training model worldwide. We are driven by a single vision: to make every aspiring engineer an industry-ready engineer.
