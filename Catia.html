CATIA Training - CDTC

“A comprehensive CATIA course that spans from basic modelling and assemblies to complex surface design, sheet metal, and digital mock-up. Tailored for aerospace, automotive and industrial design applications.”

Skills Covered

  • 3D part design and parametric modeling
  • Assembly creation & constraints
  • Surface modeling for complex geometries
  • Drafting & 2D drawing generation
  • Sheet metal and generative shape design
  • Introductory analysis and simulation

Who should enroll?

  • Mechanical / Aerospace / Automotive students
  • Design professionals seeking PLM integration
  • Teams in product development & R&D

Why CDTC?

  • Industry-oriented CATIA projects
  • Faculty with multi-domain CAD expertise
  • Certification & placement support

Syllabus Snapshot

Modules can be customized; here's a typical outline.

Module 1 - Introduction & Sketcher
  • CATIA interface, navigation, workbenches
  • 2D sketching, constraints, dimensions
  • Best practices for parametric design
Module 2 - Part Design
  • Extrude, pad, shaft, pocket, fillet, pattern
  • Parametric control & feature-based modeling
Module 3 - Assembly Design
  • Bottom-up & top-down assembly creation
  • Assembly constraints, interference checks
  • Exploded views & assembly management
Module 4 - Drafting & Documentation
  • Creating 2D drawings from 3D models
  • Dimensioning, annotations & BOM

Prerequisites

  • A suitable CAD Lab/Classroom/Auditorium with projector and seating must be arranged.
  • The CAD Lab having licensed software (AutoCAD, SolidWorks,CATIA, Creo, NX/UG) will be an added advantage; however, the training program can be conducted smoothly even without it.
