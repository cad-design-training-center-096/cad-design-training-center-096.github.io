Creo (PTC) Training - CDTC

Comprehensive PTC Creo training covering 3D part design, assemblies, drafting, surfacing, sheet metal, and introductory simulation — tailored for aerospace, automotive, and manufacturing industries.

Skills Covered

  • 3D part modeling & parametric design
  • Assembly creation & interference checks
  • Drafting, GD&T & drawing management
  • Surface modeling & freeform geometry
  • Sheet metal design & manufacturing prep
  • Intro to simulation & CAE basics

Who should enroll?

  • Mechanical, Aerospace, Automotive students
  • Design & manufacturing engineers
  • Industry professionals seeking Creo expertise

Why CDTC?

  • Hands-on projects with real-world relevance
  • Faculty experienced in CAD/CAM & PLM
  • Certification, portfolio & placement support

Syllabus Snapshot

Modules can be customized; typical outline below.

Module 1 - Creo Fundamentals
  • Interface, navigation & workbenches
  • Sketching, constraints & parametric basics
Module 2 - Part Modeling
  • Extrude, revolve, holes, rounds, patterns
  • Feature-based & parametric modeling
Module 3 - Assembly Design
  • Bottom-up & top-down assemblies
  • Assembly constraints & interference checks
  • Large assembly management
Module 4 - Drafting & GD&T
  • Creating 2D drawings from 3D models
  • Annotations, GD&T & BOM
Module 5 - Surface & Advanced Design
  • Wireframe & surface modeling
  • Freeform & styling surfaces
Module 6 - Sheet Metal & Simulation
  • Sheet metal design, bends & flat patterns
  • Introductory simulation & analysis
