Privacy Policy-CDTC (Central Development & Training Center)

Effective Date: 10th November 2025

At CDTC (Central Development & Training Center) (“Company”, “we”, “our”), we respect and protect the privacy of our students, institutional partners, industry collaborators, and visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal and institutional information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you engage with CDTC's services:

  • Personal Information: Name, contact number, email address, postal address.
  • Institutional Information: College/School name, department details, designation of faculty or institutional head, billing information.
  • Student Information: Name, course, branch/discipline, year of study.
  • Industry Client Information: Company name, GST details, billing information, technical queries.
  • Technical Data: Training files, coding, models, project submissions, and related data shared during training or consulting projects.
  • Website/Online Data: IP address, browser type, cookies, and interactions with our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data will be used strictly for professional and educational purposes:

  • To deliver training and educational programs.
  • To coordinate with institutions, faculty, and students.
  • To provide technical consultancy to small-scale industries.
  • To issue certificates, reports, and invoices.
  • To improve our training programs, digital platforms, and communication.
  • To ensure security and compliance with legal requirements.

We do not sell or rent your personal or institutional data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We may share your information only in the following cases:

  • With institutional partners (schools, colleges, industries) where you are enrolled for training.
  • With authorized trainers, staff, or consultants of CDTC.
  • When required by law, government, or regulatory bodies.
  • For certificate verification upon student or employer request.

4. Intellectual Property & Data Protection

All Training models, coding, training content, and course material remain the intellectual property of CDTC unless otherwise agreed. Any project files or proprietary data shared by industries/clients will be kept strictly confidential and used only for the intended purpose of training or consulting. Contributors, students, and partners are prohibited from copying, distributing, or misusing CDTC’s training material, Training files, or branding assets without permission.

5. Payment & Financial Data

We may collect and store billing-related data (like GST details, UPI/Bank transfer confirmation, invoices) for accounting purposes. CDTC does not store sensitive payment details like debit/credit card numbers.

6. Cookies & Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to improve user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration. However, no online system is 100% secure, and CDTC is not liable for breaches beyond our reasonable control.

8. Retention of Data

Student, institutional, and client records are retained only for as long as necessary for training, certification, and legal compliance. Technical Training files shared for project-based training are deleted upon course/project completion unless explicitly required.

9. Your Rights

You may request:

  • Access to the data we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of your personal data (subject to legal/institutional requirements).
  • Limiting the use of your information for marketing or communication.

10. Updates to Privacy Policy

CDTC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on our website, and the “Effective Date” will be revised.
