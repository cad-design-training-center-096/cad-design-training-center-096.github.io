SolidWorks Training - CDTC

“Unlock the power of parametric 3D design using SolidWorks - cover part modelling, assemblies, drawing generation and simulation workflows. Perfect for product designers, mechanical engineers and prototyping teams.”

Skills Covered

  • Parametric part modelling and design intent
  • Advanced assembly techniques, mates & configurations
  • Detail drawings with balloons, BOMs and revision control
  • Sheet metal design, weldments, and hole tables
  • Surface modelling, lofts, sweeps & surfacing strategies
  • Intro to Simulation (FEA) and Motion studies

Who should enroll?

  • Mechanical engineering students (B.Tech / Diploma)
  • Designers moving from 2D to 3D workflows
  • Manufacturing, R&D, and product development teams

Why CDTC?

  • Project-based training with industry examples
  • Faculty with hands-on CAD/CAM experience
  • Certificate & placement support

Syllabus Snapshot

Tailored per institute; below is a typical module breakdown.

Module 1 — SolidWorks Fundamentals
  • Interface, sketching best practices, relations & constraints
  • Part modelling basics: extrude, revolve, hole wizard, fillets
  • Design intent & parametric practices
Module 2 - Assemblies & Design Management
  • Top-down vs bottom-up modelling, smart mates, interference detection
  • Configurations, design tables & large assembly tools
Module 3 - Drawings & Documentation
  • Drawing templates, views, section views, annotations & BOMs
  • Revision management & tolerance practices
Module 4 - Sheet Metal, Weldments & Manufacturing
  • Sheet metal features, flat patterns, bend tables
  • Weldment structural members, cut lists & profiles
  • Basic CAM export workflows

Prerequisites

  • A suitable CAD Lab/Classroom/Auditorium with projector and seating must be arranged.
  • The CAD Lab having licensed software (AutoCAD, SolidWorks,CATIA, Creo, NX/UG) will be an added advantage; however, the training program can be conducted smoothly even without it.
