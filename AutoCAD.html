Syllabus Snapshot

Modules can be customized; typical outline below.

Module 1 - AutoCAD Fundamentals
  • Interface, navigation & workspaces
  • Basic drawing & editing commands
Module 2 - 2D Drafting
  • Lines, polylines, circles, arcs, polygons
  • Layers, blocks & dimensioning
Module 3 - 3D Modeling
  • 3D primitives & solid modeling
  • Extrude, revolve, sweep, loft
  • View controls & navigation
Module 4 - Layouts & Annotations
  • Paper space & model space
  • Plotting & scaling drawings
  • Annotations, hatching & symbols

Prerequisites

  • A suitable CAD Lab/Classroom/Auditorium with projector and seating must be arranged.
  • The CAD Lab having licensed software (AutoCAD, SolidWorks,CATIA, Creo, NX/UG) will be an added advantage; however, the training program can be conducted smoothly even without it.
