CDTC offers training programs across multiple technology domains including Robotics, CAD Design, Coding & Programming, Artificial Intelligence, Advanced Microsoft Office, and Agentic AI-based Digital Productivity Tools. Institutions can select the most suitable program for their students based on their academic needs.

Students gain practical exposure to modern/STEM technologies used in real industries. The training focuses on building creativity, problem-solving ability, technology awareness, and confidence in using industry-relevant digital tools.

CDTC programs are designed for school students, college students, and institutions looking to introduce modern technology skills early in education. No prior technical knowledge is required as the training starts with fundamental concepts.

CDTC typically conducts focused training sessions which are delivered directly on campus in collaboration with partner institutions. Custom sessions can also be arranged based on institutional requirements.

The training is designed to be practical and interactive. Students learn through demonstrations, guided exercises, and real-world examples that help them understand how technologies like Robotics, design tools, coding, artificial intelligence, and digital productivity platforms are used in industries.
